FAQ

Good to know

Does it need refrigeration?

No. Thanks to retort technology, the meal is shelf-stable for 6 months and 100% bacteria-free without any preservatives — store it at room temperature.

How do I prepare it?

Just heat the pouch in a microwave or in hot water for a couple of minutes, then pour into a bowl and eat. It's fully cooked already.

Is it vegetarian?

Yes, both flavours are 100% vegetarian and made with paneer and vegetables. Note: contains milk; may contain traces of nuts and sesame.

What is retort technology?

It's an advanced heat-sealing process that keeps food fresh and safe for long periods without preservatives or refrigeration — a first of its kind in India for meals like this.

How much protein does it have?

Each 225g pack delivers 16g of protein and 236 kcal, largely from paneer.
